Interpretation
--------------
The forward pass supports scalable bridged multicollisions and cannot provide
collision resistance. In all tested families, foldback separates the complete
message histories exactly when their differing bytes are processed in reverse.
This is finite structural evidence, not a proof of collision or preimage
resistance.
