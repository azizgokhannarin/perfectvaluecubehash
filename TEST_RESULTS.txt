Interpretation
--------------
The canonical forward transition is not injective. The tested foldback pass
separates all observed two-byte forward merges. This is a limited empirical
result, not a security proof. The next target is a constrained meet-in-the-
middle or equivalent-chain attack that satisfies both forward and foldback
conditions.
