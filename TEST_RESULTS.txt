Interpretation
--------------
Forward absorption is increasingly non-injective as reachable contexts grow.
All newly observed three-byte aliases are controller aliases that execute the
same physical moves. Foldback separated every observed alias and was injective
on the complete three-byte domain. These are finite empirical results, not a
collision-resistance or preimage-resistance proof.
